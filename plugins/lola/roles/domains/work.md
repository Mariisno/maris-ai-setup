## Who owns what
- **The human** takes the direction decisions in this domain. Decisions are put *to* them as
  variants, never made around them.
- **Named people:** product owner, tech owner, and who answers what. Write the real names
  here, not in the role files.
- **Rules of the house:** how new issues are filed (assignee, parent item, review field),
  whose issues are never touched without the human's explicit ok, which surfaces have their
  own component set.

Sources: name where each row comes from (`[internal]` with a date, `[read]` with a command),
so the next specialist can tell a fact from a guess.
