# Waiting on you

Everything here waits for **your answer**: a judgment, a choice, an approval, or something a
session stopped at a gate. It is not your task list: your own work lives wherever you track
work. Leads and specialists add rows with `lola-queue add`; you (or a lead, on your word)
close them with `lola-queue done <n> "<answer>"`. Nobody edits the tables by hand: several
sessions write here in the same minute, and the script is what keeps rows from being lost.
